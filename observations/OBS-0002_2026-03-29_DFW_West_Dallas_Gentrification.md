# DFW West Dallas / Oak Cliff — Gentrification Pricing Compression

## Market

- Dallas, TX — West Dallas / Oak Cliff (75211, 75212, 75220, 75233)

## Insight

- ARVs in 75211 and 75212 have risen sharply over the past 18 months — retail comps now reaching $230,000–$260,000 on renovated 3/2s that were at $170,000 in 2023.
- Flip buyers (like Diana Reyes) are now paying $175,000–$210,000 for deals in this corridor — higher than 12 months ago.
- However, seller expectations have also risen. Distressed sellers who previously accepted $110,000–$125,000 are now anchoring at $140,000–$160,000 after seeing their neighbors sell.
- The spread between MAO and seller expectation is compressing. Deals that used to have $20,000–$30,000 in margin are now running $10,000–$18,000.
- 75220 and 75229 are stabilizing — gentrification effect hasn't reached those ZIPs yet. Better MAO room there.
- Foundation problems are less frequent in Oak Cliff than North Houston, but older plumbing (galvanized) is a consistent repair item adding $8,000–$15,000.

## Impact

- Target 75220 and 75229 for deals with the most room — seller expectations still lagging retail.
- In 75211 and 75212 deals must be priced precisely — zero tolerance for repair surprises.
- When seller asks above MAO in 75211/75212, negotiate hard or walk. The market's heat is in the buyer's favor but not enough to absorb a blown spread.
- Add galvanized plumbing check to all intake forms for this corridor.
