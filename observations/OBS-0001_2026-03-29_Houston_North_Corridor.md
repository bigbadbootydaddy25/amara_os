# Houston North Corridor — Buyer Price Ceiling Holding at $165k–$180k

## Market

- Houston, TX — North Corridor (77008, 77009, 77018, 77022, 77026)

## Insight

- Active cash buyers (specifically Marcus Webb and comparable operators) are consistently paying $150,000–$180,000 for 3/2 SFRs in 77008–77022 with $25k–$40k repair scopes.
- ARVs in this corridor are running $200,000–$230,000 post-rehab based on Feb–Mar 2026 comp activity.
- At those buyer prices and repair levels, MAO range is $110,000–$135,000 for a $15,000 assignment fee.
- Sellers in the corridor are still anchored to pre-correction pricing ($130k–$145k asking) — creating a narrow but workable spread.
- Foundation issues in 77022 and 77026 are common (pier and beam construction, 1950s–1970s stock). Buyers discount $15k–$25k for active foundation movement.

## Impact

- Deals in this corridor must come in at $119,000–$135,000 to clear $15,000 fee.
- Any property with foundation issues needs $10,000–$15,000 added to repair estimate before calculating MAO.
- Do not assume ARV above $225,000 for sub-1,200 sqft properties — retail market softened slightly in Q4 2025.
- Buyer interest is high — North Houston remains one of the most liquid wholesale corridors in the state.

## Action

- Set MAO floor at $119,000 for standard 3/2, 1,100–1,300 sqft, $30k–$40k repairs in this corridor.
- Add foundation flag to all deals in 77022 and 77026 during property intake.
- Confirm with Marcus Webb on any deal above $135k contract price before going under contract.
