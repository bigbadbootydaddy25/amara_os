# Phoenix West Corridor — High Buyer Velocity, Repair Cost Inflation

## Market

- Phoenix, AZ — West Phoenix / Glendale (85009, 85031, 85033, 85035, 85037, 85301, 85302, 85303)

## Insight

- West Phoenix is the most active wholesale corridor in the Phoenix metro. Cash buyer volume is high — multiple operators buying 2+ deals per month in a single ZIP.
- ARVs have remained stable in the $290,000–$340,000 range for 3/2 SFRs (1,200–1,500 sqft) as of Q1 2026, recovering from a brief dip in mid-2025.
- Buyer prices (what investors pay as-is) are running $230,000–$270,000 for standard rehab deals. This supports MAOs of $170,000–$210,000 at $15k assignment fee and $40k–$50k repairs.
- Repair costs in Phoenix have risen approximately 18% since 2023 driven by labor costs. HVAC replacements (common in Phoenix — average lifespan is shorter due to heat stress) are running $8,000–$12,000 versus $5,000–$7,000 two years ago. Factor this into every Phoenix repair estimate.
- Sellers in 85031 and 85033 are frequently absentee investors who accumulated in 2019–2021 and are now looking to exit — motivated, reasonable on price.
- Competition from other wholesalers in this corridor is real. Carlos Mendez and buyers like him receive multiple deals per week. Lead with clean numbers and fast communication.

## Impact

- Phoenix repair estimates need a 15–20% buffer over Houston or DFW equivalents for the same scope of work.
- HVAC should always be a line item in Phoenix — assume replacement unless unit is less than 5 years old.
- In 85031 and 85033 specifically, absentee-owner outreach has strong conversion potential. These sellers often have no emotional attachment to the property.
- Buyer competition in Phoenix means deals move fast — confirm buyer interest within 24 hours of making seller contact, or another wholesaler will.

## Action

- Apply 18% repair cost multiplier to all Phoenix deal estimates vs. baseline.
- Always include HVAC replacement ($10,000) as a repair line item in Phoenix unless property inspection confirms otherwise.
- Prioritize 85031 and 85033 for direct-to-seller outreach — absentee owner profiles, high motivated seller density.
- Confirm Carlos Mendez's interest within 24 hours on any West Phoenix deal — do not sit on a live deal in this corridor.
