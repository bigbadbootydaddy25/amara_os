## Executive Summary

Pre-foreclosure outreach is the practice of contacting a property owner **after a trustee-sale notice has been filed but before the auction date**. The goal is to help the owner avoid foreclosure (and the credit damage that accompanies it) by negotiating a purchase, short sale, or loan modification — while the investor acquires the property at a discount.

For pre-foreclosure outreach in Texas to be effective, the source must provide:

1. **Owner Name** — to address communications personally and to locate them if they have moved
2. **Property Address** — the address of the distressed property
3. **Sufficient Lead Time** — at least 20+ days before the auction to make contact, negotiate, and close
4. **Opening Bid** — to understand the lender's floor and the homeowner's remaining equity position
5. **Ideally: Mailing Address** — the owner's current mailing address (may differ from property address)

Texas's 21-day notice requirement creates a predictable window each month for pre-foreclosure outreach. The five sources ranked below are the best for sourcing early-notice, owner-identified leads.

## Pre-Foreclosure Outreach Best Practices for Texas

### Direct Mail — Primary Channel

Direct mail remains the highest-response outreach channel for pre-foreclosure leads. Key elements:

- **Personalized salutation** using the owner name from the trustee-sale notice
- **Empathetic tone** — avoid language that feels predatory or exploitative
- **Clear offer** — a specific, simple proposal (e.g., "I'd like to buy your home for cash, as-is")
- **Easy response mechanism** — phone number + website + QR code
- **Timing** — first letter arrives within 10–12 days of notice filing; follow-up 7–10 days later

### Phone Outreach — Secondary Channel

If a phone number can be obtained through skip-tracing:
- Call during business hours on Days 14–18
- Keep the call brief and empathetic
- Focus on the homeowner's situation, not your investment goals
- Have a clear elevator pitch ready

### Door Knocking — High-Priority Properties Only

Reserve for properties that haven't responded to mail or phone, where the opportunity is particularly compelling:
- Highest equity situations
- Properties you've identified as owner-occupied based on mail delivery confirmation or utility data
- Days 20–24 (one week before auction)

### What Makes Texas Pre-Foreclosure Different

- **No right of redemption** after the trustee sale — the homeowner has zero recourse once the gavel falls, which creates urgency
- **Short timeline (21–28 days)** — outreach must begin immediately upon notice filing
- **First Tuesday is fixed and public** — both investor and homeowner know exactly when the deadline is, which concentrates motivation in the final week
