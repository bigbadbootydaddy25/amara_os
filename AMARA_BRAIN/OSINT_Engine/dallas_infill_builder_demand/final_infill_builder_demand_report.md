## CRITICAL POLICY STACK — WHY 2025-2026 IS THE WINDOW

The following policy stack, all enacted in the 2024-2026 window, creates conditions for Dallas infill that have not existed in 40 years:

1. **ForwardDallas 2.0 (September 2024)** — First comprehensive land use update since 2006. All rezoning cases now cite this as policy justification, accelerating infill approvals.

2. **Missing Middle "1-8 Dwelling Units" Ordinance (April 23, 2025)** — Buildings up to 8 units / 3 stories now build under IRC (residential code) instead of IBC (commercial code). This collapses construction costs. Projects that previously couldn't pencil can now.

3. **Parking Minimums Removed (May 2025)** — Eliminates a major site constraint on small infill lots.

4. **South Dallas/Fair Park Area Plan (June 2025)** — Creates explicit entitlement pathways, ADU rights, and duplex subdivision rights in 75215/75210.

5. **West Oak Cliff Rezoning Cascade (2022–2026+)** — 35+ acres of auto-oriented commercial converting to mixed-use. Each rezoning creates new adjacent residential infill pressure.

6. **Dallas Comprehensive Zoning Code Rewrite (Expected 2026)** — First full code update in ~40 years. Will further systematize infill approval pathways.

7. **2025 Texas State Law** — Limits what municipalities can regulate regarding multifamily height and setbacks, reducing community opposition tools.

8. **DallasNow Permit Portal (May 2025)** — 88% reduction in permit review time. Infill projects now permitted in 8 days vs. 68 days. Speed is a builder buying decision — faster permitting = more willingness to acquire lots.

**The window: builders who understand this policy stack are moving NOW. AMARA OS needs to acquire and place distressed infill lots BEFORE the general market reprices them to reflect the new development economics.**
