## WHY THIS MATTERS

Under 11 U.S.C. § 362, the filing of a bankruptcy petition creates an **automatic stay** that immediately halts:
- All collection actions against the debtor or debtor's property
- All foreclosure and tax sale proceedings
- All liens, judgments, or transfers of property

If the owner filed bankruptcy **before the tax sale date**, the tax sale may be **void ab initio** (void from the beginning), even if the sale already occurred. Buying a property whose tax sale was conducted in violation of an automatic stay exposes the purchaser to significant legal risk.

Additionally, under 11 U.S.C. § 542-543, a bankruptcy trustee may recover property (including tax sale purchases) made within certain look-back periods as fraudulent or preferential transfers.
