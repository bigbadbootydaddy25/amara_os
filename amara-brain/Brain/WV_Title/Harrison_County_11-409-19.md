### Key Lessons Learned

1. **TAGIS parcel-ID fails in Elk-Outside** — always use coordinate-radius for well search
2. **WV split estate** = mineral reservation in a conveyance deed, not a separate mineral deed
3. **Harrison County Sheriff** = surface tax only — mineral accounts held by Assessor separately
4. **Pre-1909 deed books** not in IDX digital system — confirm from recitals in later deeds
5. **White Space tracts** — read vesting deed recitals completely — chain is embedded in the deed body
6. **Entity name discrepancy** Inc. vs LP is common with mineral aggregators — always flag and research both
7. **Sheriff system URL** is `http://` only — https fails silently
8. **Burns deed DB 1197/1258** contains the surface chain completely — read all 5 pages
9. **Bijou Coal and Hope Gas** unreleased encumbrances — both pre-digital — neither released of record
