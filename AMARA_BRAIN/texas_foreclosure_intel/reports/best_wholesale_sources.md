## Executive Summary

Wholesale real estate investing in Texas foreclosures depends on **identifying distressed properties early, making contact with the owner before the auction, and either controlling the deal through assignment or buying at the courthouse steps below market value.** The best sources for wholesale leads must provide: property address, owner name, and sufficient lead time (at minimum 21 days before the auction) to make contact and structure a deal.

The five sources ranked below were selected based on:
- Lead volume (number of properties listed per month)
- Data completeness (address + owner name + opening bid + sale date)
- Lead time before auction (21+ days preferred)
- Accessibility (free vs. subscription)
- Multi-county coverage

## Rank 1 — BDF Group (Barrett Daffin Frappier Turner & Engel LLP)

**Website:** https://www.bdfgroup.com/foreclosure-posting/  
**Source Type:** Trustee Law Firm  
**Counties Covered:** Harris, Dallas, Tarrant (and additional Texas counties)  
**Verification Status:** VERIFIED  
**Cost:** Free — public access, no login required  

### Why It's #1 for Wholesale

BDF Group is the **single highest-volume source of pre-auction foreclosure leads in Texas**. As the largest substitute trustee law firm in the state, BDF Group processes thousands of trustee-sale notices per month across all three major Texas counties. Their website posts downloadable county-specific PDFs containing every upcoming sale they are handling.

**What You Get:**
- Property address (full street address)
- Owner/borrower name
- Opening bid amount
- Sale date (first Tuesday)
- Trustee information
- Lender / servicer name (varies)
- Instrument reference (deed of trust)

**Lead Volume:** Hundreds to thousands of properties per month across Harris/Dallas/Tarrant combined  
**Lead Time:** 20–25 days before the first Tuesday auction  
**Automation Score:** 8/10  

### Why It's Ideal for Wholesale

BDF Group notices appear **20–25 days before the sale date**, giving a wholesale investor a workable window to:
1. Skip-trace the owner through HCAD/DCAD/TAD for their current mailing address
2. Send a direct mail letter or make a phone call
3. Negotiate a deed-in-lieu or purchase agreement
4. Either close on the property before auction or assign the contract

The opening bid shown on BDF Group postings also reveals the **minimum the lender will accept at auction**, giving investors a clear ceiling for pre-auction offer negotiation.

### Action Steps
1. Download the Harris, Dallas, and Tarrant County PDFs from bdfgroup.com on the 5th–8th of each month
2. Parse all addresses and owner names
3. Cross-reference against HCAD/DCAD/TAD for owner mailing address and property values
4. Filter for properties with equity (assessed value minus opening bid > target margin)
5. Launch direct mail or outreach campaign immediately
6. Follow up one week before the auction date

## Rank 3 — Dallas County Clerk Official Foreclosure Notices

**Website:** https://www.dallascounty.org/departments/countyclerk/foreclosures.php  
**Source Type:** Official County Source  
**Counties Covered:** Dallas County  
**Verification Status:** VERIFIED  
**Cost:** Free — public access  

### Why It's #3 for Wholesale

The Dallas County Clerk's Office publishes the **complete, authoritative list** of all trustee-sale notices filed in Dallas County each month. Unlike the trustee firm websites (which only show that firm's cases), the county clerk list covers **every trustee firm** operating in Dallas County. This means you capture BDF Group, Mackie Wolf, Buczek Enterprises, and every other trustee all in one document.

**What You Get:**
- Complete coverage of all Dallas County foreclosures — no gaps between firms
- Property address
- Owner/borrower name
- Opening bid
- Sale date
- Trustee / law firm name
- Instrument reference

**Lead Time:** 20–25 days before auction  
**Automation Score:** 7/10  

### Why It's Ideal for Wholesale

This is the most comprehensive single source for Dallas County. For a Dallas-focused wholesale operation, this one source provides 100% coverage of upcoming trustee sales. The trade-off is that it is Dallas-only — you need separate sources for Harris and Tarrant.

### Action Steps
1. Monitor dallascounty.org/departments/countyclerk/foreclosures.php monthly for new PDF posting
2. Download the monthly notice PDF immediately upon posting (typically first 5–8 days of month)
3. Parse all entries for address, owner, opening bid, sale date
4. Enrich with DCAD for owner mailing address and assessed value
5. Filter for equity deals (assessed value significantly above opening bid)
6. Launch direct mail and phone outreach
